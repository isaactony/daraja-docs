A **shortcode** is the number that identifies a business on M-Pesa. Which kind you have changes how you fill in several fields.

## Kinds of shortcode

| Kind | What customers see | Account number? | Typical use |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **Paybill** | "Lipa na M-Pesa → Pay Bill", business number plus account number | Yes, `BillRefNumber` or `AccountReference` | Utilities, schools, online shops that track invoices |
| **Till** (Buy Goods) | "Lipa na M-Pesa → Buy Goods", till number only | No | Shops and restaurants |
| **Store number** (head office) | Not shown to customers | No | Groups tills under one account; used for go-live and as `BusinessShortCode` for till STK Push |
| **B2C shortcode** (bulk disbursement) | The sender name on the customer's SMS | No | Paying out money with B2C |

Safaricom can also set up one account that both receives and sends money. The official B2C page calls this a "one account".

## STK Push fields for each kind

| Field | Paybill | Till |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| `TransactionType` | `CustomerPayBillOnline` | `CustomerBuyGoodsOnline` |
| `BusinessShortCode` | The paybill number | The store number (head office) used on go-live |
| `PartyB` | The paybill number | The till number |
| `AccountReference` | The account number the customer pays to, up to 12 characters | Still required; shown in the prompt |

The official Go Live section asks for the **store number** when you go live with a till, which is why it, not the till number, is your `BusinessShortCode`. Developers often report till integrations that worked in the sandbox with `174379` and then failed in production because they used `CustomerPayBillOnline` or sent the till number as `BusinessShortCode`.

<Note>
  The sandbox test shortcode `174379` is a paybill. You can't test the till variant fully in the sandbox.
</Note>

## C2B fields for each kind

When a customer pays you from their M-Pesa menu, the C2B notification's `TransactionType` is `Pay Bill` or `Buy Goods`. `BillRefNumber` holds the account number for paybills and is usually empty for tills. See [Receive paybill and till payments](/guides/receive-paybill-and-till-payments).

## Shortcode lengths

The official docs give different lengths on different pages: 5 to 6 digits, 5 to 7 digits, and 6 to 9 digits. Don't validate shortcodes by length in your code. See [Known doc discrepancies](/reference/known-doc-discrepancies).
