Going live attaches your sandbox app to a real paybill, till, or B2C shortcode. Safaricom then emails you production details and moves the app to production.

## What you need first

Most go-live delays are business setup, not code. Get these ready before you start:

- **A live M-Pesa shortcode:** a paybill, a till with its store number, or a B2C (bulk disbursement) shortcode.
- **Access to the M-Pesa organization portal** at [org.ke.m-pesa.com](https://org.ke.m-pesa.com), with a user who has the **Business Administrator** or **Business Manager** role. Access requires a head office set up for your shortcode. Contact M-PESABusiness@safaricom.co.ke for the forms.
- **That user's phone number must be a Safaricom line.** The go-live one-time password is sent to the phone number on their portal profile.
- For B2C and Transaction Status: an **API operator (initiator)** with the right API roles, created on the organization portal.

## Go-live steps

1. In the Daraja portal, open your app and go to **Go Live**.
2. Enter the **organization shortcode**: the paybill number, the store number for a till, the head office number, or the B2C shortcode.
3. Enter the **organization name**, shortened, with no symbols or special characters.
4. Enter the **M-Pesa username** of the Business Administrator or Manager. It's case-sensitive.
5. Enter the one-time password sent to that user's phone.

The official FAQ says app approval takes up to 24 working hours (Monday to Friday, 8 AM to 5 PM). Safaricom then emails the production details, including the passkey if your app has M-Pesa Express, and the app moves to production with new keys.

## Change your configuration

| Setting | Production value |
| :-- | :-- |
| Base URL | `https://api.safaricom.co.ke` |
| Consumer key and secret | The production app's keys |
| Shortcode and passkey | Your real shortcode, and the passkey from the email |
| `TransactionType` | `CustomerBuyGoodsOnline` if you're a till. See [Paybills, tills, and shortcodes](/concepts/paybill-till-and-shortcodes). |
| Certificate | The **production** certificate, for security credentials |
| Initiator | Your production API operator and password |
| Callback URLs | HTTPS, on your production domain |

## Checklist

- [ ] All callback URLs use HTTPS, are publicly reachable, and contain no blocked words.
- [ ] Your firewall allows Safaricom's callback IP addresses. See [Callbacks](/concepts/callbacks).
- [ ] C2B URLs are final. Production registration is one-time.
- [ ] Tokens are cached and shared across servers. See [Get an access token](/guides/get-an-access-token).
- [ ] A scheduled job resolves pending payments with STK Query or Transaction Status.
- [ ] Callback handlers ignore duplicates and check amounts.
- [ ] You've made one small real payment end to end, and seen it in the organization portal.
